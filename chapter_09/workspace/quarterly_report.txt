Quarterly report, Q3

Summary
Revenue grew 12% over the previous quarter, driven by the new team plan.
Churn fell from 4.1% to 3.6%. Support response time rose from 5 to 7 hours
because ticket volume grew faster than the support team.

Highlights
- The team plan reached 1,200 paying accounts in its first full quarter.
- The mobile app rating improved from 4.1 to 4.4 after the offline mode release.
- Infrastructure costs stayed flat despite 18% more usage.

Concerns
- Support is understaffed for the current ticket volume.
- Two enterprise customers asked for single sign-on before renewing in Q1.
- The billing system migration is three weeks behind schedule.

Next quarter
The leadership team agreed to hire two support engineers, to start single
sign-on work in October so it ships before the Q1 renewals, and to bring the
billing migration back on schedule by moving one engineer from the reporting
project. Finance will review the pricing of the team plan in November.
